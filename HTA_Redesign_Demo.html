About the studio

Run by animators, not account managers.

Happy Trails Animation is led by owner/producer Andy Collen from Portland, Oregon, working with a rotating crew of animators, illustrators, and sound designers on each project.

  • 30+ years producing animation for entertainment, publishing, and marketing clients
  • Independent short films recognized at Cannes, Annecy, and the Telluride, Melbourne & Seattle festivals
  • Direct, hands-on collaboration — you work with the people actually making your animation
